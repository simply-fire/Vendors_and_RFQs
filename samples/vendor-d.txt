Vendor Profile — Aerospace NDT Specialist

Company: AeroInspect Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
Location: Bengaluru, Karnataka
Established: 2014
Employees: 42

CAPABILITIES

Specialised aerospace inspection and non-destructive testing services for aluminium and titanium components, including structural brackets, machined housings and flight hardware.

In-house NDT capabilities include dye penetrant inspection (DPI) to ASTM E1417 and magnetic particle inspection (MPI) to ASTM E1444 where applicable. Inspection procedures are supported by controlled work instructions and calibrated inspection equipment.

Experienced in processing batches of up to 250–300 aerospace components per month. Typical turnaround for standard inspection lots is 3–5 working days.

QUALITY

NADCAP accredited for Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), with accreditation covering applicable penetrant and magnetic particle inspection processes.

ISO 9001:2015 certified.

Employs qualified Level II NDT technicians and one Level III NDT specialist responsible for procedure approval, personnel qualification and technical oversight.

Digital inspection reports are maintained against component serial numbers, with inspection history retained for traceability.

Inspection equipment is calibrated through accredited laboratories, with current calibration certificates maintained within the quality system.

CAPACITY AND DELIVERY

Two-shift operation, six days per week. Current utilisation approximately 65%.

Available capacity of approximately 300 components per month, subject to component mix and inspection complexity.

Standard turnaround is 3–5 working days, with expedited processing available for urgent aerospace requirements.

EXPERIENCE

Approximately 8 years of aerospace inspection experience, including aluminium and titanium structural components.

Current customers include aerospace component manufacturers and defence engineering suppliers.

Limited direct experience with space programme components, although the company has supported inspection activities for defence-related aerospace hardware.
